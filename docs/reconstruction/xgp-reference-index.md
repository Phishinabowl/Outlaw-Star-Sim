## Selected individual observations

- img022: printed heading identifies cockpit/navigation in operation; apparatus lies behind the seat group. Printed top number 8 differs from the inset cockpit view label. Preserve both during detailed indexing.
- img030: engine-room drawing has four circular units in 2×2 arrangement. Lower-left assembly is extended with exposed controls; do not claim all four are extended in this sheet. The callout identifies an engine control cylinder; exact relationship to exterior engines requires anime corroboration.
- img043: rest-room rough drawing explicitly marked 第一稿 (first draft). Do not promote it to approved final-design evidence. It can explain design evolution.
- img044: main passage has directions toward cockpit and dining room, an exterior-access floor hatch, an air-conditioner label, and maintenance-robot guide rail. These are local relationships, not a complete deck plan.
- img088/089: レストルーム means rest room/lounge here, not evidence of a toilet. Two configurations show convertible furniture; img088 calls out four floor panels raised/lowered to become a table or bed. Cockpit direction arrow is visible.
- img125: Assault Shooter drawing shows telescoping deployment, a capsule and hatch, and an exterior mounting relationship. Do not turn this into a traversable corridor design before reviewing anime operation.
- img126: airlock interior shows hatch/pressure-display states and sliding direction. Fine annotations remain pending.
- img100: built-in equipment/weapons overview adds later architecture constraints; combat implementation is deferred.
