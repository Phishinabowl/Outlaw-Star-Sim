## Remaining Local Spatial Proposals

### Cockpit Canopy And Walking Clearance

Test a shaped canopy instead of a uniformly taller room. Within the 8 × 6 m cockpit trial,
keep the central 4 m transverse band at the existing 2.30 m overhead trial through the moving
seat/apparatus region, approximately local length 0.95–7.5 m. Test sloping shoulders down to
about 2.10 m over the two outer 1 m bands. These are clear-space targets before fittings, not
a specified hull skin, measured anime profile or final mesh. Front/rear transitions remain shaped
around the actual source landmarks and doorway; do not create abrupt overhead ledges in walking paths.

The [EP-04 headroom review](../../reference/indexes/episode-04-cockpit-headroom-review.md)
supports higher central canopy and lower perimeter framing qualitatively. The proposed band widths
and heights are unmeasured. The 4 m band covers the 3.6 m trial seat width with only 0.2 m per side;
it does not automatically contain projecting controls or all cover travel. MC-01/04 must include them.

Use a nominal 1.80 m standing player proxy for the first fit proposal, independent of assigning
the player Gene's identity. Preserve separately qualified character heights; Jim's translated 1.40 m
statement and secondary Gene/Melfina values are not a universal avatar scale. Check feet-to-head,
stance, boots, carried equipment and camera, with an initial 0.20 m standing-head clearance target.
This E margin is a test target, not a comfort guarantee or a source measurement. Replace the proxy
with the selected body in Phase 3; rerun affected checks rather than silently shortening it.

Rail placement remains a protected overhead/service region, not a rail drawn through that head
clearance. At the raised seat and cylinder, compare actual rail carrier/body and source-shaped canopy
together. If they cannot coexist, revise the local profile/routing hypothesis and record the compromise;
neither a robot-free ceiling assumption nor the 2.30 m example is a fit pass.

### Engineering Platform And Upper Controls

Use the current service floor as Z=0 and propose a continuous 1.20 m usable central platform/route
from the forward operator area toward the bank. Terminate normal circulation before fixed central
equipment; no walk-through behind the bank. Preserve the source platform's recognizable edges,
pipes and any required local steps when fitting the proxy. The existing 3.0 m local machinery-height
trial remains a consequence to test, not an accepted ceiling or an expansion of the 16 m exterior envelope.

Do not add an upper catwalk or ladder in this proposal. The 2.10 m upper-row center does not identify
the actual handle/control height. Record the source-shaped control positions, selected standing
surface and body reach in MC-03. All four required local actions must be reachable in the chosen
states; no remote Gilliam completion or player/cylinder collision exception may hide a failure.

If upper controls fail reach, reopen the bank/platform proposal before implementing them. Compare
source proportions and fixed equipment first; then review a local step/platform change or explicit
control adaptation with the user. That contingency is an iteration point, not a selected raised
platform. Detailed reach/input/motion accommodation belongs to Phase 1.3 and later occupied tests.

### Portable Computer Storage

Propose a closed computer secured in a player-worn side holster, initially on the player's left hip,
after disconnection. This avoids adding a permanent ship cabinet or leaving the computer on the
moving seat assembly. Keep the cable contained with it; no dangling cable crosses the cap or seat sweep.
The back-mounted trunk remains a separate carried item and is placed before cockpit work.

Start with a 0.40 × 0.30 × 0.08 m closed-computer/holster proxy. This is an E equipment-fit trial,
not a measurement of Hilda's device. Revise it against the selected source-shaped prop. Include the
added lateral depth during ladders, hatch entry, ordinary walking, seating and both camera views.
If a worn holster fails, revisit attachment/storage rather than allowing equipment to disappear
through the ship. Exact stow input, animations and equipped-item presentation belong to 1.3;
this does not add a general inventory screen, arbitrary item slots or weight simulation.
