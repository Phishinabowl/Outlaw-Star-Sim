Test an apparatus cavity beneath the rear cockpit, roughly X=16–18.5 m, with an initial 2.5 × 2.5 m
reservation in plan and about 2.5 m depth below the main floor. These are deliberately conservative
E boxes for M02/M03, not tank diameter, shaft stroke or proof the forward hull has that depth.

connector terminates outside that footprint rather than passing beneath it.

only a test band, not new evidence for the hatch's location. Neither Z=-2.5 m nor the official overall
height fixes its hangar-floor height, boarding jump or pressure boundary.

   move/shrink ordinary connecting space first if the body narrows, preserving recognizable machinery.
2. Test MC-01/02 for apparatus cavity, seat lift, occupied headroom, demo case anchor and temporary
   computer workspace. Keep the case's agreed player-blocking exception explicit; no equivalent
   exception is assumed for hull, seats or apparatus.
3. Test loaded entry/H03 ascent, hatch sweep/landing and MC-05/06 against grappler roots; revise
   the lower-route station/shape if the provisional entrance band conflicts with those reservations.
4. Test MC-03/04 with all four cylinders extended, reachable controls, service platform and a
   continuous escape path. First target at least 1.2 m clear operator circulation where practical,
   an E test margin rather than a source requirement or guaranteed fit.
5. Preserve M10–M13 and MC-07/08 reservations where affected. The approach/dining allocation
   is not permission to occupy Shooter storage, lounge furniture or support/landing-mechanism space.
