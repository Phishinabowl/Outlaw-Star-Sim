## Initial Route

Use one continuous central corridor connecting the forward cockpit to aft engineering. The required
round trip stays in that corridor and its necessary engineering approach; it does not enter a kitchen,
dining or common room. Preserve the existing boarding/lower-entry/H03 connection proposal and
separate cockpit apparatus cavity, subject to their existing evidence and fit limits.

Represent a future kitchen/dining connection with one closed side-hatch placeholder along the
corridor. Its position is adjustable; do not assign a fixed side, longitudinal station, room footprint
or confirmed source-hatch identity. No room behind it needs to be built or entered for the initial
route proof. The placeholder does not imply an opening animation or new interaction.

"Continuous corridor" defines the intended connection, not a proved ruler-straight passage, fixed
length or final deck plan. Necessary local bends/offsets remain fit decisions. Do not alter the
canonical overall hull envelope, player scale or machinery reservations to force a straight line.

## Deferred Placement And Fit

Kitchen/dining room placement, size, doorway correspondence and relationship to the lounge/rest
drawings remain deferred until the basic hull/corridor geometry provides usable sizing information.
Compare room options against actual hull cross-sections, circulation, structure and machinery
reservations before selecting a room. No full room shell, fixtures, cooking or furniture motion is
required by this amendment. M13's future furniture reservation remains applicable if later room
placement overlaps its candidate region; a closed hatch does not demonstrate that room's fit.

The former X=31–37 m dining-shell band and 6 × 4.5 m footprint are superseded as initial room
requirements. That portion of the former route can carry corridor/approach space as needed;
neither the kitchen nor its hatch inherits those coordinates. Existing cockpit and engineering
trial bands retain their provisional status. Reconcile affected allocations during later authorized
route work rather than treating this amendment as a new dimensional proof.

## Evidence And Decision Boundary

SET-044 and SET-045 have opposing cockpit/dining viewpoint and destination labels while using
nearly identical wall-hatch, piping, floor-hatch and opening compositions. They establish named
directions, but their geometric relationship remains unresolved. Do not treat them as independently
demonstrated opposite ends of a fully reconstructed passage. Whether the middle wall hatch connects
to the room in preliminary SET-043 is an unverified interpretation, not an adopted source identity.

The corridor/side-room direction is an accepted E reconstruction choice, not a new canon finding.
The original walk-through dining agreement remains historical in the Phase 1.2 packet, Layout A
and closeout; this amendment governs the changed initial requirement. Other startup mechanics,
readiness conditions and creative staging remain unchanged.

## Execution And Acceptance

The implementation plan and VS1-G02 procedure use cockpit → central corridor → engineering →
cockpit, with the closed kitchen/dining side-hatch placeholder kept clear of the active route and
reserved machinery. Validate body/camera traversal and occupied machinery clearances in the
existing later checkpoints. Kitchen-room fit is explicitly untested and deferred.

This update authorizes documentation only. Phase 3.2A and later modeling/gameplay remain unstarted
and require their existing execution authorization. No additional phase gate or new test ID is added.
