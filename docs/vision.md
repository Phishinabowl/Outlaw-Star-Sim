# Vision

Working title: Outlaw Star XGP Simulator. The XGP itself is the primary experience.

Eventually explore a coherent ship in first and third person, enter from outside, open doors/hatches/panels, sit at stations, operate interconnected systems, and start the dormant ship. Engineering, four main engines, navigation, power, cooling, sensors, communications, life support, grapplers, and sub-ether travel should have meaningful state.

Gilliam will operate the same authoritative command/state interface as physical consoles. Future maintenance robots, flight, external cameras, faults, rerouting, repairs, component damage, and grappler combat are extensibility requirements, not Phase 0 deliverables.

Initial experience: dark asteroid storage hangar; sealed, mostly powered-down ship; limited emergency power; physical entry; staged startup; engine unsealing; bridge activation; eventual departure. Sub-ether travel will use a game-appropriate transition, not literal relativistic simulation.

Accepted planning envelope: 72 m long × 21 m wide × 16 m high. These dimensions are supplied by the handover brief; primary/official provenance remains to be located.
