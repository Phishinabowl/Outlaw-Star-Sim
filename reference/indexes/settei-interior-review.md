# Detailed settei review — interior and startup batch

Reviewed 2026-10-06. This first focused batch covers 16 representative scans from the 57 XGP candidates. It is not completion of the full detailed collection audit. Repeated scan subjects are not assumed identical; manifest hashes contain no byte-identical duplicates among all 221 scans. Later copies/variants need comparison.

Full-image visual review, with manual transcription of legible selected labels; no OCR/automatic translation. `high` = clear selected text; `medium` = provisional handwriting reading. Not every callout is transcribed. PRODUCTION_CANON candidates remain subject to collection provenance; visible 決定稿 means final-design marking on the scan, not independent authentication. SET-043 is preliminary and excluded from final-design classification. No metric values were derived from perspective ratios.

## Reconstruction constraints

- SET-006 gives approximately 2 m floor-to-ceiling height for ship sections. Treat as an explicit production convention to test against animation and machinery; it does not establish deck count or guarantee every unusual bay shares the height.
- SET-040 supplies an approximately 8.5 cm comparison cigarette beside the startup key. This is a local scale cue; perspective does not justify scaling an entire room from it.
- SET-027 provides physical stages for the navigation apparatus. The floor opening and downward occupant motion require space below the bridge floor; its extent is unresolved.
- SET-044/045 connect passage directions to cockpit/dining and label an Assault Tube access ladder. SET-046 separately points toward dining. These are evidence-backed relationships, not a measured adjacency/deck plan.
- SET-030 explicitly calls its protruding assembly an engine control cylinder. Keep that distinction from exterior propulsion hardware when analyzing startup.
- SET-088/089 support convertible furniture; SET-043 is an earlier rough design and must not silently supply missing final geometry.
- SET-047/126 provide both access mechanism and grappler-relative positioning. The passage floor hatch may be a different opening; keep them separate pending anime comparison.
