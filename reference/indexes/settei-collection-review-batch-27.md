# Broader Settei Collection Review — Batch 27

Reviewed 2026-10-25. Individual inspection of SET-150–159 enriches the
[screening CSV](settei-screening.csv). Each original was viewed separately. Original-map wording
and immutable scans remain intact. Selected readings are not full translations; production
authenticity remains unverified. Explicit Angel Links headings and contextual AL attribution are
distinguished, as are final-design markings and approval not recorded. No episode verification,
modeling, art generation or Phase 3.2A work occurred. Fictional source mechanisms do not add
gameplay requirements or establish real engineering feasibility.
