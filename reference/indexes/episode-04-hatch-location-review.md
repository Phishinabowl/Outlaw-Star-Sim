## Conclusion And Remaining Alternatives

The lower-hull interpretation is supported. The airlock-between-grapplers relationship is a useful
constraint; the exact H04/H05 match remains provisional despite strong shared features. A working
candidate is a ventral body entry in the grappler region. The inspected material does not establish
that it lies in the rear propulsion/engineering region or that engineering is the closer interior destination.

The whole exterior settei suggest testing the forward/mid-body grappler region first (D qualitative
landmark inference), but no percentage along the 72 m hull, port/starboard side or height is selected.
Perspective compression, cropped frames and stowed assemblies prevent a defensible metric placement
from this pass. The user's aft recollection remains an alternative to check, not silently discarded.

A hatch below the cockpit does not by itself prove it is below the entire main passage. Compare a
lower entry section with a local rise toward the passage against a passage that already occupies a
lower level and rises farther forward. Neither requires asserting a full extra inhabited deck.

Future E route proposal must reconcile the hull envelope, SET-126 grappler-root reservation, M08/M09,
round floor access H03, and M02/M03 navigation cavity. A convenient lower access volume must not
consume Melfina's shaft, the suspected floor-hatch access volume or future arm machinery. Carrying the
back-mounted trunk/handheld computer through any level transition must be reviewed explicitly.

No route-length preference, final location, metric dimension, pressure simulation, model or gameplay
implementation is chosen. PNGs/logs remain local-only/ignored; all authored changes remain uncommitted.
