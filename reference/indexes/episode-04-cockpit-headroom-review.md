## Observations And Projection Limits

The 07:15 composition shows all four crew standing around the lowered seating, overhead Gilliam,
a central canopy/glazing recess and its lower perimeter framing. It supports modeling a shaped
ceiling with local clearances, rather than assuming one horizontal plane across the cockpit.
Gene is nearly full-height but his footwear extends to the lower frame boundary; Melfina's lower
legs are cropped and Jim is partly occluded. Hilda is farther forward than Gene/Jim.

These characters and ceiling edges are not all at the same camera depth. Spiked hair, boot soles,
posture, cropped feet and different floor/ceiling projection points matter. The nearest visible roof
edge above a head is not automatically vertically above that person's floor contact. A direct
head-to-screen-edge pixel ratio would manufacture precision. No defensible metric ceiling height
was recovered from these three frames alone, and no vanishing-point/camera solution was fitted.

SET-011 is useful for relative silhouettes/scale checking, not a ruler in metres. Its 1996 date also
requires comparing with later character designs before enforcing every proportion. Do not assume
that hair-tip height, bare-body height and booted collision height are interchangeable.
