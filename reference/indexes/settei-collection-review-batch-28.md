# Broader Settei Collection Review — Batch 28

Reviewed 2026-10-25. Individual inspection of SET-160–169 enriches the
[screening CSV](settei-screening.csv). Each original was viewed separately. Original-map wording,
prior records and immutable scans remain intact. Selected readings are not full translations;
production authenticity remains unverified. Related compositions remain independently indexed,
and later final markings do not retroactively approve earlier drafts. No episode verification,
modeling, art generation or Phase 3.2A work occurred. Source guidance does not add gameplay scope.
