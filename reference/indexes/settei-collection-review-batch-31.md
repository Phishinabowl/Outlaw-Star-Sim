# Broader Settei Collection Review — Batch 31

Reviewed 2026-10-25. Individual inspection of SET-190–199 enriches the
[screening CSV](settei-screening.csv). Each original was viewed separately. Original-map wording,
prior records and immutable scans remain intact. Selected readings are not full translations;
final-design markings do not independently authenticate production provenance. Repeated designs
remain individually indexed. Engineering destination arrows do not resolve the full ship layout.
No modeling, art generation or Phase 3.2A work occurred.

## SET-190 — Heifong company main-store exterior

Source: [_source_settei/img190.jpg](../../_source_settei/img190.jpg).

- **#10〜11 羅公司 ヘイフォン本店 ① 外観** identifies the **Heifong main store exterior**. The company reading is ローカンパニー; an official Latin rendering is not established here.

- **決定稿**, SUNRISE, **1997.8.05** and **10-7** are visible. No numerical building dimensions are established.
- Related composition appears in [SET-146](settei-collection-review-batch-26.md#SET-146). Retain the individual scan; matching composition does not establish identical annotations or bytes.

## SET-197 — Exterior airlock hatch and control-cover motion

Source: [_source_settei/img197.jpg](../../_source_settei/img197.jpg).

- **⑰ エアロック** identifies the **airlock**, shown on the exterior hull with an open recess and Gene for scale.
- The hatch note explicitly says it **first recesses inward, then slides**. This is useful mechanical-motion evidence, not a complete dimensioned track design.
- The control panel has a **separate cover that slides downward**, with light and keypad details. Do not combine that cover motion with the main hatch sequence.
- **ポジション・マーカー** and **艦底のライン** identify a position marker and hull-bottom reference line. Exact installation dimensions remain unstated.
- **決定稿 1997.5.27**, inset **97.5.26**. Prior [SET-047 follow-up](startup-open-items-followup.md) and related [SET-126](settei-collection-review-batch-24.md#SET-126) are retained; no exact scan identity is asserted.
