[screening CSV](settei-screening.csv). Each original was viewed separately. Original-map wording,
prior selected findings and immutable scans remain intact. Selected readings are not full
translations; production authenticity remains unverified. First drafts, final-design markings,
conflicting dates/context labels and approval not recorded remain distinct. No episode verification,
modeling, art generation or Phase 3.2A work occurred. Source guidance does not expand gameplay scope.
