# Broader Settei Collection Review — Batch 33

Reviewed 2026-10-25. Final batch covers SET-210–221, including the two remaining scans,
and enriches the [screening CSV](settei-screening.csv). Each original was viewed separately.
Original-map wording, prior records and immutable scans remain intact. Selected readings are
not full translations; final-design markings do not independently authenticate production provenance.
First drafts and drawing references retain their distinct status. Future HQ direction is carried
forward without resolving site/version questions. No modeling or Phase 3.2A work occurred.
