# Broader Settei Collection Review — Batch 24

Reviewed 2026-10-25. Individual inspection of SET-120–129 enriches the
[screening CSV](settei-screening.csv). Each original was viewed separately. Original-map wording,
prior selected callouts and immutable scans remain intact. Selected readings are not full translations;
production authenticity remains unverified. First drafts, final-design markings and approval not
recorded are distinguished. No episode verification, modeling, art generation or Phase 3.2A work
occurred. Source production guidance does not add gameplay requirements.
