# Broader Settei Collection Review — Batch 25

Reviewed 2026-10-25. Individual inspection of SET-130–139 enriches the
[screening CSV](settei-screening.csv). Each original was viewed separately. Prior findings,
original-map wording and immutable scans remain intact. Selected readings are not full translations;
production authenticity remains unverified. Visible final-design stamps do not resolve conflicting
ownership labels or rough annotations. No episode verification, modeling, art generation or
Phase 3.2A work occurred. Source production guidance does not add gameplay requirements.

## SET-136 — Caster gun, cartridge and loading sequence

Source: [_source_settei/img136.jpg](../../_source_settei/img136.jpg).

- **古式銃**, with **キャスター** reading, identifies the **caster / old-style gun**, high confidence. Firing note explicitly names Gene.
- Large side and front views detail barrel ornament, trigger guard and grip. Numbered loading instructions lower the lock handle, slide the assembly open, push in a cartridge, return the slide and raise the handle to lock.
- Cooling/acceleration rings emit light through slits and begin rotating as the trigger is pulled; rotation peaks at discharge, high confidence for selected instruction. No numeric rotation speed or timing is supplied.
- Cartridge base uses a stylized **kanji numeral matching its shell number**; example **3** is drawn. **決定稿**, SUNRISE and **97.3.21** are visible; inset **97.3.20**, **#1 onward**.
- Original-map caster identity is supported. The recorded caster-first preference stays deferred; ammunition scarcity, shell effects, damage and other gameplay values are not established here.

## SET-139 — Gene's double-barrel shotgun and forceful loading mechanism

Source: [_source_settei/img139.jpg](../../_source_settei/img139.jpg).

- **ジーンの銃C** and **ジーンの2連ショットガン** — **Gene's gun C / double-barrel shotgun**, high confidence. Note identifies this as the gun carried in Gene's holster.
- Large side/front views and Gene's holstered silhouette show the paired barrels, grip and scale relationship. Text allows firing the barrels **alternately or together**, high confidence.
- Mechanism inset labels **air damper**, **release button** and ejection component. Notes call for considerable force during loading and a forceful loading-gate opening during ejection.
- Another instruction specifies pointing it **downward during cartridge ejection**, high confidence for selected guidance. **決定稿**, SUNRISE and **97.3.03** are visible; inset also says **rough**.
- Original-map weapon identity is supported. Frequency of use, relative firepower and caster-shell scarcity remain historical leads rather than verified findings from this sheet.
