## Apparatus Versus Lower Access — First Fit Concept

Re-inspected SET-022/028 and SET-044/126 after the user questioned whether the lower entry is
too far aft to threaten Melfina's cavity. The cockpit is forward/dorsal in the exterior references;
the ventral grappler-region entrance is consistent with being aft of that cockpit (qualitative D),
but no measured separation or near-engineering location is established.

The more specific local distinction is visible in the room designs: SET-022/028 place the apparatus
behind the seats within the cockpit, while SET-044 places H03 in the passage outside the doorway
labeled cockpit. These are separate floor openings on opposite sides of the proposed cockpit boundary;
their apparent perspective separation does not give a reliable metre distance.

Thus the external entrance itself need not share the apparatus's underfloor volume. The potential
conflict arises only if the chosen lower connecting passage or H03 ascent is routed into that cavity.
Test this E concept first:

1. Keep the M02/M03 storage/descent envelope beneath the apparatus's cockpit footprint.
2. Keep the H03 ascent/landing envelope on the passage side of the cockpit boundary.
3. Route the lower access toward H03 beneath the passage, ending before the cockpit cavity;
   connect back toward the lower exterior entrance without claiming a full lower deck.

This is a separation strategy, not a demonstrated fit or selected connector mechanism. Later metric
proposal must show structural/clearance margins between both volumes, usable trunk/body/hand space,
headroom through H03 and sufficient hull clearance. It must also avoid grappler-root reservations.
Do not move H03 far aft merely to remove a conflict while claiming fidelity to SET-044's doorway view.
