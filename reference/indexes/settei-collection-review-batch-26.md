# Broader Settei Collection Review — Batch 26

Reviewed 2026-10-25. Individual inspection of SET-140–149 enriches the
[screening CSV](settei-screening.csv). Each original was viewed separately. Original-map wording,
prior records and immutable scans remain intact. Selected readings are not full translations;
production authenticity remains unverified. Rough designs, production lists and final-design
markings are distinguished. SET-144 explicitly belongs to Angel Links. No episode verification,
modeling, art generation or Phase 3.2A work occurred. Source production guidance does not add
gameplay requirements.

<a id="SET-140"></a>
## SET-140 — Gene's interchangeable-barrel blaster concept

Source: [_source_settei/img140.jpg](../../_source_settei/img140.jpg).

- **ジーンの銃D** and **ジーンのブラスター銃** — **Gene's gun D / blaster gun**, high confidence. **ラフ**, rough, is visible; no final-design stamp or date recorded.
- Large revolver-like design has a star-marked grip, fold-down trigger, **ball controller** and a separate blaster cartridge. Standard, long and short barrel arrangements are shown.
- Selected guidance contrasts long-barrel faster/more precise fire with lower power against short-barrel more powerful but slower/less precise fire. Ball-controller sketches show curved projectile paths, high confidence for qualitative guidance.
- Barrel exchange is intended to show different uses/fighting styles. Cartridge caliber text and crossed-out wording remain unresolved; no numerical performance or finished weapon implementation is adopted.
- Keep separate from the final-marked [caster sheet SET-136](settei-collection-review-batch-25.md#SET-136). This rough concept does not resolve Gene's complete final arsenal.
