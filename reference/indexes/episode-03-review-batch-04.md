## Findings

Eight contiguous groups cover the rest conversation, planet/moon approach, rendezvous
search and wreckage, pirate ambush/Ron exchange, gunner argument, childhood memory,
gunner handoff and missile exchange. Image 275 is FLASHBACK; the other 71 are STORY.

Images 217–234 preserve Hilda's reclined pilot posture and the three passenger seats.
The standing crew in 224 and seated arrangement in 225 are useful staging references;
Jim's satchel contents remain unknown. The conversation describes the Leyline, pirates'
treasure interest and the MacDougalls' work, without establishing visible hardware or
universal operating rules from those statements.

Images 223, 245 and 285 preserve complementary Horus hull angles, dark pods, yellow
cone units and the attached underside dropship. Plumes and streaked backgrounds do
not establish measured speed, universal drive cooldown or a complete activation cycle.
Images 235–239 show a green banded planet and cratered rendezvous moon; neither is
assigned a proper name or equated with Farfallas. Relative composition is not a scale
measurement. Image 241 preserves Melfina's reach toward an auxiliary-station grip.
Image 246 is particularly useful for the pilot sightline, crosshair/grid viewing surface
and small overhead screen; hologram construction and sensor specifications remain unknown.

Dialogue identifies the combined wreckage as Leprechaun and Purgatory. Image 248 shows
broken cylindrical sections and panels, but does not let individual pieces or readable
nose art be assigned independently to one vessel. The reported killing of Hilda's
friends is retained as bounded dialogue/synopsis context, not a body or casualty count.

Image 254 matches the orange-and-blue pirate mothership design already indexed in
Episodes 1 and 4. Image 255 shows fighters and an open launch-tube mouth; 258 and 283
show the green fighters in formation. The established pirate_fighter tag is retained;
the translated term "cub fighters" is contextual wording, not a newly verified model
specification or an automatic identification with the separate Episode 4 landing units.

Images 256–265 preserve the female pirate leader, Ron's lower costume and clear face
view, gold railings, blue floor lights and the room's large viewing surface. Horus in
261–262 is display imagery; the ship is not physically in that room. The female leader's
Soi Len name lead remains provisional. Image 284 similarly retains the descriptive
elderly pirate leader ID; the Roi Fong lead is not promoted. Both leaders' appearances
are useful even while those secondary name-to-face associations remain unresolved.

Images 267–268 and 279 show the cockpit entry relative to the pilot and standing Gene.
Image 279 is a shirt/collar grip, not an aimed weapon; nearby round wall hardware and
its lever remain functionally unassigned. Image 275 shows young Gene behind reflected
light in the established childhood escape-memory continuity. His father and a complete
escape-pod layout are not visible. Image 276 returns to the adult close-up.

Images 281–282 establish changed gunner-seat occupancy: Jim seated/Gene standing,
then Gene seated/Jim behind-right. They preserve cream grips, side controls, overhead
hardware and operator reach without establishing every intermediate adjustment.
Image 286 supplies the operating posture. Nearby dialogue says lasers will not work
against the approaching fighters and orders missiles; this does not define an unlimited
weapon-immunity rule. Image 287 is especially valuable for the square multi-tube missile
bank, departing red-tipped missile and smoke/trail effects. Tube count, reload mechanism,
control mapping and guidance remain unverified. Image 288 shows curved trails among
fighters without proving each hit, casualty or later damage result.
