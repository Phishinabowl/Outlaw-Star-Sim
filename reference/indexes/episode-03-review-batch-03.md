## Findings

Nine contiguous scene groups continue from batch 02: right-of-way confrontation, close
passage, arrest order/Horus acceleration, Aisha's firing sequence, Horus departure,
mid-episode eyecatch, Blue Heaven exterior, McCoy questioning/home orders and onward route.

The translated shield-drop order, three-degree port change and ten-second starboard-thruster
burn concern Orta-Honehone. They do not describe Horus or the XGP. Dialogue says that dropping
shields permits passage, but the sampled stills do not establish the shield's construction,
precise boundary, physical clearance or complete maneuver. Images 160 and 165 show bright
blue-white effects near the large hull; exact nozzle assignment and the full burn cycle
remain unverified. Images 167 and 170 preserve useful Horus side profiles alongside the
large hull without providing a measured scale ratio or proving contact.

Images 152 and 154 show Horus on oval display surfaces. Image 154 preserves Aisha's chair,
the large main display and lower crew stations. Images 158–159 and 207–211 supply red
curved console, oval-display, uniform and braid references. Display imagery does not place
the depicted craft in the room or establish sensor type/range. The waterfall/vegetation/rock
environment remains a shipboard setting of unverified construction. Images 184–185 provide
wide command-space and full-body Aisha costume references, including lower-leg wraps,
trousers, braid, shoulder armor and bell. Character expressions are not biological or damage
specifications. Individual crew names and ranks remain unassigned.

Image 163 shows Jim bracing over the gunner console. Image 181 shows Melfina bracing at
the auxiliary station with an extended arm. These retain reach/clearance information without
assigning acceleration forces or every control function. Horus's reporting voice is not
labeled Gilliam. Image 176 shows a raised oval dome on a pale pursuer; its function remains
unverified. McCoy in 145 and the unnamed long-haired pilot in 173 remain distinct.

The translated firing order belongs to Aisha. Images 189–191 preserve Orta-Honehone and
yellow beam imagery without identifying the source of every streak or proving a hit on Horus.
Image 196 shows a yellow forward burst, dark/gold streaks and bright blue-white separated
trails behind Horus. Maintainer clarification on 2026-10-29 identifies this as the continuous
sub-ether activation/jump animation, with 197 showing the now-empty departure space after
Horus blips away. This supersedes the unresolved event interpretation. The clarification is
retained as reported sequence context, not a new independent video inspection. Image 196
remains high-value for future transition-effect work; exact timing, trail evolution and drive
mechanics require targeted review. Its direct alignment match verifies timing identity, not
those mechanics. Image 197 is not a measured transit-duration or route-distance record.

Images 198–200 preserve three mid-episode graphic states, including a clean logo and
divided circular emblem; complete animation/audio timing is outside this survey.
Images 201–202 show Blue Heaven with readable cyan signs, towers, antenna structures,
attached rocks and Orta-Honehone below. Precise berth, docking attachment and dimensions
remain unverified. Images 204–205 show McCoy within Aisha's command environment; the
stills do not establish every intervening event or the cause of dark patches in his hair.
Hilda's reputation, Aisha's pursuit demand and the home order to remain at Blue Heaven
are dialogue context. Aisha's later demotion is not asserted as occurring in these images.

Image 214 is a clear Horus forward-oblique exterior with the smaller attached underside
craft identified through Horus dropship continuity. Nearby dialogue reports three further
sub-ether legs and a 2 h 10 min wait until the next drive on this route. These are not
universal jump, speed or cooldown specifications.
