## Findings and limits

Fifteen contiguous scene groups distinguish docking support, the entrance, disembarking,
greetings, the bluff, Swanzo's explanation, Jim interaction, Horus detail inserts, corridor travel,
shopping arrangements, market, fitting, performer insert and bar conversation. Individual images
retain separate observations, feature tags, priorities and uncertainty fields in the category index.

- 217–219 show main Horus and an overhead rail carriage with articulated support rods. At 219
  it hangs beneath that assembly beside the Private Gate sign. The stills establish support
  configurations, not a complete clamp animation, load path or docking procedure.
- 220–221 preserve the rocky WELCOME entrance, bridges, ring structures and colored lights.
  These do not establish a force field, station dimensions or an airtightness model.
- 222 provides a useful side view of suspended Horus in the hangar. 223 shows the wheeled,
  enclosed boarding stairway, cab, stabilizing feet and open lower doorway. 224 shows the
  travelers at that exit. Exact stairway actuation and chassis propulsion remain unverified.
- 228–244 establish Swanzo's short turquoise rounded exterior, red harness/pouches, thin limbs,
  white gloves, contrasting shoes and visor. The translated dialogue and cached prose identify
  Swanzo; he is not Gilliam or an unidentified maintenance robot. The tall human companion is
  retained as unnamed in image tags: the prose names partner Mikey but supplies no appearance
  match. This is not an identification of McCoy, who is discussed as absent.
- 245–247 show Gene's purple blaster beside Swanzo and then lifted away. Gene says it is
  unloaded and that he is bluffing. No discharge or independent ammunition inspection is shown
  in these stills. The subsequent warning about a bullet breaking a seal and losing the town's
  air is dialogue context, not a measured pressure-system specification. Swanzo's three-power
  explanation similarly does not define an exhaustive faction simulation.
- 255–259 preserve Swanzo's close interaction with Jim. At 258 Jim's face is reflected across
  the curved turquoise front, beneath Swanzo's own black visor and yellow dots. Do not read this
  as Jim inside the suit or use it to infer alien physiology or a specific optical construction.
- 260–262 are high-value Horus exterior inserts: spherical forward hull and circular hatch,
  panel seams/scuffs, colored couplings, rear pylon vent grids, gold cone hardware and overhead
  support. The repair dialogue does not assign a cause to every visible scuff or verify a repair
  method. These are main-Horus references, not XGP surfaces or a separate dropship identity.
- 263–273 show Blue Heaven's warm-lit station corridor. Yellow lighting changes the appearance
  of the existing costumes; it is not evidence of new brown outfits. 271 preserves Melfina's
  travel-suit back assembly, Hilda's holstered pistol, octagonal doorframe and small side controls.
  272 adds repeating ceiling arches, handrails and corridor depth. No ship-room adjacency follows.
- 274–278 preserve the multilevel market, shopfronts, bridges, signage, vendor and boutique.
  Translated dialogue calls the offered food meat pies; exact ingredients are not established.
  Only readable English signage is recorded. The boutique's complete proper name and unclear
  neighboring text are not assigned from cropped lettering.
- 279–283 show Melfina's clothing fitting: white collar and tie, purple garment with pale blue
  shoulder panels and three round green ornaments, gray tights, black shoes and gold ankle ring.
  SET-011 already records a maintainer-supplied going-out-outfit context; these Episode 2 images
  provide separate usage references. Neither the scale chart nor these perspectives supply metric
  heights or a complete tailoring specification. The fitting-room clerk remains unnamed.
- 284–285 show a female pole performer under red and blue light. The insert is retained for
  costume/lighting research without assigning a name or a particular screen/projection technology.
  286–288 begin Gene and Hilda's bar conversation; 287 is a strong room/staging reference with
  table seating, drinks, plate, fringed lamp and foreground patrons. Later hiring scenes remain
  outside this image batch.
