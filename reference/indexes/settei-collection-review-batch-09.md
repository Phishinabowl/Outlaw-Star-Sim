## SET-033 — Maintenance Robot, First Draft

Source: [_source_settei/img033.jpg](../../_source_settei/img033.jpg).

- Heading: **アウトロースター号 ⑭ メンテロボ** — **Outlaw Star (14), maintenance robot**,
  high confidence. Inset also identifies a maintenance robot.
- Closed/open body views show eyes/brows, shell separation, extending manipulators, interchangeable
  tip examples, bottom roller and a rail-mounted roller assembly. A beverage can provides a drawn
  scale comparison; no metric robot size is calculated from it.
- Stamp: **第一稿** — **First draft**, high confidence; visible date **97.4.19**. The generic
  production-canon candidate classification from thumbnail screening is corrected to preliminary
  material, not final-design evidence. This does not discard the drawing's reference value.
- Selected note: **#4〜8はこの顔なしバージョンでお願いします。** — **Use this version
  without a face for episodes 4–8**, high confidence. This is production guidance attached to a
  variant drawing, not confirmation of what actually appears in those episodes.
- The original-map Gilliam identity, low-gravity utility and lifting recollections remain attributed
  leads. The drawing establishes neither lifting capacity nor a fully traced ship-wide rail system.
  Later robot sheets still need individual comparison; full callout transcription remains open.
